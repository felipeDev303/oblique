Bring your papers

Results of the experiment: one call against one idea at a time, and the map of the deck.

Each PDF is read on its own into a sheet. Three research ideas are then written from the sheets one at a time, each reading the ones before it and, with cards on, under a different oblique card. Each idea shows its card and the passages it cites.
