# Evolution G1

The record of how the `OB-G1-*` cards were written (ADR-010).

- `call.json`: the call to Muse Wonder and its response, verbatim (run R-20261101-01).
- `validation.json`: the checks on the 15 proposed cards.

The author reviewed the proposal under ADR-002 and discarded OB-G1-011 and OB-G1-014, with no replacements. The other 13 are in `deck/cards/` with their proposed IDs and text, which is why the numbering has gaps. Both discarded cards appear in the two files above only because those files record the proposal as it was; they are not part of the deck.
