# Evolution G1

The record of how the `OB-G1-*` cards were written (ADR-010).

- `call.json`: the call to Muse Wonder and its response, verbatim (run R-20261101-01).
- `validation.json`: the checks on the 15 proposed cards.

The author reviewed the proposal under ADR-002 and discarded two cards, with no replacements:

- OB-G1-011: too close to cards of *Oblique Strategies* (Eno and Schmidt), which ADR-002 keeps out of this deck.
- OB-G1-014: it almost repeated E6-2.

The other 13 are in `deck/cards/` with their proposed IDs and text, which is why the numbering has gaps.

The text of both discarded cards remains in the two files above and in [`runs/R-20261101-01/summary.md`](../../../runs/R-20261101-01/summary.md), because those files record the proposal as it was. They are not part of the deck, and they were never measured.
